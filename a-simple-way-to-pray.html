A Simple Way to Pray

Daily prayer, rooted in Martin Luther's letter of the same name.

Guided prayer offices from morning to night, catechism memorization, and a community prayer wall. Completely free, with no subscriptions or paywalls.

What's Inside

Everything in the app is free, and always will be.

Daily Prayer Offices

Morning, Midday, Evening, Close of Day, and Night Watch devotions, each with psalms, Scripture readings, and prayers drawn from the historic liturgy.

Prayer Weaver

Build your own prayer from the four strands Luther taught: instruction, thanksgiving, confession, and petition.

Catechism Memorization

Work through the Small Catechism piece by piece with a memorization tool built for steady, repeated practice.

Prayer Wall

Submit your own prayer requests, and pull real requests from the community to pray over and fulfill.

Theological Studies

In-depth confessional Lutheran studies on the Trinity and the Nicene Creed, written to be read slowly and returned to often.
