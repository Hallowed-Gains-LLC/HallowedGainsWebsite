Early Bloom

A gentle companion for a pregnancy where the baby may arrive early, and for the baby, whenever they get here.

Follow the pregnancy week by week, keep track of scans, medicines and symptoms in one place, keep family posted, and switch to baby care and NICU guidance once your baby has arrived.

What's Inside

Clear, sourced information for the weeks that matter, and simple tools for keeping track of what's happening.

Week by Week

The exact week and day, how big baby is and what usually happens this week, with preterm-aware tips. From 22 weeks, if baby arrived today: what a birth this week would likely mean, from large studies. You can hide the survival statistics at any time.

Your Conditions

Guides to preeclampsia, HELLP, short cervix, waters breaking early (PPROM), placenta previa, accreta and abruption, growth restriction, gestational diabetes, cholestasis, twins and a previous preterm birth: what each means for monitoring and birth, the warning signs to call about, and questions to ask.

Scans & Baby's Growth

Copy in each scan's results to see every measurement's centile on INTERGROWTH-21st or Hadlock charts, and baby's estimated weight today between scans. For twins and triplets, each baby by name, a plan based on whether they share a placenta, and how far apart their weights are.

Journal & Care-Team Report

Log contractions, blood pressure, blood sugar, bleeding, symptoms, mood and notes, and count kicks live. Add photos of pads to a bleed: they stay out of your gallery, and only you and your partner see them. Keep a list of questions, and email a report to your care team from your own email app.

Hospital Stays & C-Sections

Who's in hospital and since when, and a shared list of things to bring. Count down to a planned C-section or induction, with your due date still in sight, then six weeks of recovery tips.

After the Birth

Baby's age three ways: actual, corrected and postmenstrual. Log feeds, pumping and diapers, timed live or afterwards, with daily totals and a milk stash. Growth charts made for babies born early, then WHO by corrected age; medicines with reminders on both parents' phones, appointments, vaccines, and NICU days and firsts.

Home Screen Widgets

The week and the days to go at a glance, in one cell or one row, then the days together once your baby is here. They update themselves at midnight. Family who follow your feed can add one too, to see how far along you are.

Keep Family Posted

Write the day's update once and send it by text or email. Or invite family to a feed of just what you share, photos included, in the app or any web browser. They get a notification for each update, leave a heart, and see how far along you are.

Sync & Partner Sharing

Optional sync through your Google account backs your journal up and brings it back on a new phone. Your partner joins with an invite, and you both see and add to everything; family can view the journal too. It's end-to-end encrypted: we can't read it, and neither can Google.

Support for Your Family

Helplines and charities for pregnancy, the NICU and loss, one tap to call or text. Work, leave and money basics for US families, gentle guidance on comfort care and saying goodbye, and a guide for dads, co-moms, grandparents and friends.

Where It Comes From

Written from published guidance by ACOG, SMFM, NICE, ISUOG, the CDC and the AAP, and from NICHD research. Every source is listed in the app. Early Bloom isn't affiliated with or endorsed by these organizations.

Prayers & Scripture

Optional, for those who want them: a verse for each day and a Lutheran prayer for where your family is, from waiting and worry to the NICU, going home and thanksgiving.

A Look Inside

Screenshots from the app, with a made-up family.

Private by Design

Early Bloom keeps what you enter on your phone, with no ads and no analytics. Sync is optional, and end-to-end encrypted when you use it: only your family's phones can read your journal. Without it, nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it, or through Android's own encrypted backup.
