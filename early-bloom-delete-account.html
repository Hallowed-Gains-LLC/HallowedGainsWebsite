Delete Your Early Bloom Account and Data

This page explains how to delete your account and data in Early Bloom, the Android app made by Hallowed Gains (Hallowed Gains LLC), what is deleted, and what is kept.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Early Bloom.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account and cloud data and confirm.

This deletes your account, the encrypted cloud copy of your data and the key copy in your Google Drive (see below).

Delete without the app

  1. From the Google account you signed in to Early Bloom with, email contact@hallowedgains.com.
  2. Use the subject “Delete my Early Bloom account”.
  3. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

You can also remove the key copy from your Google Drive yourself: in Google Drive, open Settings → Manage apps → Early Bloom → Options → Delete hidden app data.

Who has an account

Early Bloom only creates an account if you turn on optional cloud sync by signing in with Google. If you never sign in, you have no account: everything stays on your phone, and Settings → Delete all data (or uninstalling the app) removes it.

What is deleted

  • Your Early Bloom account: your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your cloud data: the end-to-end encrypted copy of your pregnancy and baby data stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Your encryption key copy: the copy of your key kept in the hidden Early Bloom folder of your own Google Drive.

If you share with a partner, your partner keeps their own copy on their own phone and in their own account unless they delete it too. Data on your phone is removed with Settings → Delete all data or by uninstalling the app.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept after deletion. The one exception is that Google's infrastructure, which Early Bloom's cloud data runs on, may keep encrypted backups for a limited time before they are permanently erased. Your data is end-to-end encrypted, so Hallowed Gains can't read it either way.
